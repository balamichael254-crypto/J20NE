# Flower photography

Real photographs, not illustrations or generated art. Sourced from Unsplash and
downloaded through Unsplash's own `/download` endpoint.

## Licence

All photographs here are covered by the [Unsplash
Licence](https://unsplash.com/license): free to use for commercial and
non-commercial purposes, no permission or attribution required. Attribution is
recorded anyway, because knowing where an asset came from matters when it needs
replacing.

Each file is traceable back to its source page at
`https://unsplash.com/photos/<id>`.

## Cut-outs

`lily-1.webp` … `lily-6.webp` and `bouquet-cutout.webp` are sliced and trimmed
from `assets/lily-sprites.png` and `assets/lily-bouquet.png`, which already
lived in this repository. They keep their alpha channel and are used for petal
fall, confetti and decorative accents.
